## Implemented boundary

`qualification/artifacts.py` captures a bounded prepared-bundle inventory through
nofollow, nonblocking descriptor-relative access. It rejects symlink/hardlinked or
nonregular objects, writable files/directories, invalid/missing executable role
paths and excessive depth/count/bytes. Required roles, scan policy, sorted file
paths, file modes/sizes/content digests and directory inventory form the manifest.

Verification compares a fresh scan with both the expected manifest and an
independently trusted digest. Capturing arbitrary content never approves it.
Metadata checks during and after scanning detect controlled replacement/mutation;
they do not freeze the filesystem or eliminate time-of-check/time-of-use races.
Trusted operator/ancestors/mounts remain assumptions. No content is executed.

The manifest is relocation-independent for identical modes/contents and explicitly
binds role assignments and resource bounds. Extra/missing/renamed/modified artifacts
or changed policy/roles fail verification. All descriptors close on failure.
