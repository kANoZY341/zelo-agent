# v0.12 — Bounded scheduler and Mind wake cycles

This slice continues v0.12 after durable Campaign ownership, operational state,
leases, recovery, and shared budget accounting are established.

## Goal

Allow Zelo Core to execute deliberately bounded future Mind wakes without
creating a second authority system or granting free-running autonomy.

## Scheduling model

Core owns durable Campaign schedules. Each schedule:

- belongs to exactly one Campaign;
- targets one existing Zelo conversation;
- contains one bounded prompt;
- is either one-shot or fixed-interval;
- uses a minimum recurring interval of 60 seconds;
- has a maximum of 100 logical occurrences;
- is immutable in Campaign, conversation, prompt, cadence, and max-run identity;
- may be cancelled by the authenticated operator;
- never grants merge, release, approval, or policy authority.

There is intentionally no cron language, arbitrary calendar expression, dynamic
schedule creation by Mind, or catch-up burst behavior in this slice.

## Wake authority

Before a scheduled Mind wake executes, Core must:

1. verify the Campaign and schedule are still active and due;
2. acquire the Campaign-wide `mind-wake` lease;
3. persist or resume the durable logical wake occurrence;
4. reserve one unit from the Campaign `mind_wakes` budget;
5. execute Zelo Mind with a deterministic occurrence idempotency key;
6. force any Mind-created Core task back into the same Campaign;
7. atomically commit the wake budget, complete the durable run, and advance
   schedule progress;
8. release the wake lease.

The lease token itself is never exposed through the schedule API or written to
Campaign events.

## Restart recovery

A process-local wake that was `running` at restart is marked `interrupted` and
does not consume its logical occurrence number. Its live lease and uncommitted
budget reservation are released when possible. The same occurrence may then be
resumed with:

- a newer lease fencing generation;
- a new budget reservation;
- the same deterministic Mind turn idempotency key.

This makes conversation/task creation replay-safe while preserving one durable
logical occurrence.

Budget charging and wake completion happen in the same serialized SQLite
transaction. This prevents a crash between those two mutations from charging
the same logical occurrence twice.

## Recurrence behavior

Recurring schedules advance from completion time, not from an accumulated list
of missed wall-clock ticks. If the service is offline for several intervals,
Core does not replay a burst of missed wakes after restart.

## Failure behavior

A normal bounded Mind failure consumes that logical occurrence as `failed` and
advances the schedule according to its fixed interval. Infrastructure shutdown
uses `interrupted` instead so the same occurrence remains retryable.

Budget exhaustion fails closed before Mind executes.

## Explicit non-goals

This slice does not add:

- free-running or self-created schedules;
- cron expressions or arbitrary calendars;
- parallel scheduler authority outside Zelo Core;
- Research specialist execution;
- workspace project/progress UI;
- push-to-talk voice;
- v0.13 persistent memory;
- autonomous merge, tag, release, or deployment.
