# v0.16 — Transport-neutral channel envelope foundation

## Goal

Reuse Zelo Mind's existing durable conversations and idempotent Core tasks for
future WhatsApp, email and browser-text channels. Avoid parallel conversation
implementations or treating an external provider's message as operator authority.

## Contracts delivered

The bounded text ingress envelope carries channel, opaque provider-account,
sender, thread and message keys; original text and its SHA-256; and an explicit
UTC provider timestamp. The envelope does **not** prove who sent the message,
that the message is authentic, that it belongs to Ahmad, or that its content can
authorize tasks. It has execution_authority=false.

Thread fingerprints are derived from channel/account/sender/thread identity.
Message fingerprints bind an additional provider message key. The message
identity remains stable across provider retries or edits, while the separate
text digest changes, allowing a future durable ingress ledger to reject
same-message-ID content drift. Each identity is domain separated.

Identities must be opaque 64-character lowercase hex tokens. The pure
derive_opaque_channel_key helper supplies HMAC-SHA256 derivation with domain
separation across channel, role and identifier. The future verified adapter
must supply its secret from protected per-account configuration; do not reuse
a shared global public key. Plain hashes of predictable phone numbers or email
addresses are **not** adequate pseudonyms. This contract cannot prove that
the adapter called the helper or that its source was authenticated, so those
checks remain mandatory before Core admission.

The prepared outbound intent binds channel/account/recipient/thread/turn and
exact text digest. It always has state=prepared and transport_authority=false.
It cannot claim a message was sent, delivered or read.

Both contracts enforce a strict field set, text/digest correspondence,
UTF-8 byte limits and no NUL. The ingress timestamp must be timezone-aware UTC.

## Reserved Core namespace

The operator-facing POST /conversations/{id}/turns route rejects both
schedule: and channel: idempotency-key prefixes. The future Core-verified
channel service may call MindService internally with channel-scoped keys;
external HTTP clients cannot forge those reserved turn identities.

## Not implemented or authorized

No provider webhook, signature verification, WhatsApp/Meta integration,
Microsoft/Google email access, caller identity, audio/voice-note handling,
outgoing transport, contact lookup, credential storage, durable channel account
mapping, delivery ledger or conversation admission is implemented here.
There is no new task/Worker/Tool authority, permission widening or background
presence. Model-provided source text remains untrusted data.

## Next gates

1. Provider adapter with signature verification, replay defense and keyed opaque
   identity mapping before Core admission.
2. Ahmad-controlled channel account binding, persisted conversation/thread
   relationship and inbound idempotency ledger.
3. Explicit outbound approval, delivery-state ledger and safe retries.
4. Qualified voice-note STT, browser voice and call transports as separate gates.
5. Operator channel controls in the final workspace after Core-first work.

No provider access or background sending is implied by this foundation.
