## Findings for the next design

The stdout tar interfaces are the candidate boundary. Information/field/show
commands extract temporary files; contents produces an external tar listing.
Even streaming forks copy/decompression children. Decoder memory and expanded
output are not hard-bounded by the inspected wrapper. Decompressor SIGPIPE may
be normalized to parent success, so cancellation/saturation must remain explicit
refusal regardless of the final exit code. Legacy/control and build-feature
fallback paths require separate exclusion and closure evidence.

See `reviews/media-package-reader-source-review.md` for the source-derived detail,
`reviews/media-package-reader-source-gates.json` for exact identities and counts,
and `v0.15-media-package-member-reader-design.md` for the candidate boundary.
